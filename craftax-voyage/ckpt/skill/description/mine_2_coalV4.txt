The function is about mining exactly 2 coal blocks. It first verifies that the player has a pickaxe with at least level 1. Then, it iterates twice to mine one coal block per iteration. In each iteration, it explores up to 60 steps until a coal block is visible. Once found, it mines that single block within up to 30 steps. The loop breaks early if the target amount of coal is already reached. Finally, it returns the updated state.
