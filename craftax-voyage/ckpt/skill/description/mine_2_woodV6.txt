The function is about gathering two additional units of wood. It first calculates the target inventory count by adding two to the current amount. Then, it enters a loop that continues until the target is reached. Inside the loop, it explores up to 60 steps to find a tree. Once a tree is located, it mines exactly one tree within a maximum of 30 steps. This process repeats until the desired wood quantity is achieved, and finally, the updated state is returned.
