The function mines a total of 5 coal blocks by first verifying that the player has a pickaxe of level 2 or higher. It then iteratively explores until a coal block is visible and mines one block at a time until the inventory reaches 5 coal. If the player already has some coal, it calculates the remaining amount needed and stops once the goal is met. The process logs each step including exploration, mining, and inventory updates. Finally, it returns the updated state with the completed task.
